About this car

Feast your eyes on this magnificent 1963 Rolls Royce Silver Cloud III. This is a fine example of the coveted left hand drive with A/C model and only 42,000 miles traveled since new. This Rolls Royce steel saloon body car with its more powerful motor, great suspension, and wonderful interior refinements was voted Car of the Year and Best Car in the World for 1963. Notice the twin headlight design and sumptuous body lines of this hand built beauty. A wonderful combination of luxury, power, speed, and status symbol, it represents then and now.
